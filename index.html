Moderator & Product Lead

Spotlight Challenge, GDG Ranchi  ·  DevFest Ranchi 2026  ·  Sep – Oct 2026

Case study
99+LinkedIn mentions within 24 hours of launch
105Participants across all platforms
3Simple steps from card to Networking Wall

The problem

Posters get pushed out. Nobody shares them.

Event promotion stayed inside the organizers' own network, and attendees were passive audience members instead of part of the story. One KPI: make the community visible, not just seen but felt.

The insight

People don't share posters. They share themselves.

The need underneath is recognition. Give people a reason and an easy way to be seen, and they become the distribution channel.

The solution

Three steps, no room to get lost

A dedicated page on the DevFest site: create your card, share it with a ready-made caption tagging GDG Ranchi and GDG India, then submit the post link to be considered for the Networking Wall.

Handoff

I designed it, Tech built it

I designed the whole flow: the card generator with live preview, the share step and the submission form. I handed it to the Tech team, who built the page from my design. Worked with a team of 5 including Design and Tech.

The interface, step by step

Designed so the reward is visible immediately

My design mockups; the live page was cleaner. Tap any image to enlarge.

  • Create: a live preview updates as you fill in the form. Badge and role chips mean no typing for common choices, and the personal line is optional.
  • Share: direct-share buttons, one copy box with the mandatory hashtags (they qualify a post, so they are impossible to miss), and a caption per platform.
  • Submit: a form that checks itself, with a hashtag confirmation, a confirmation email and a note that cards appear on the site after 5 days.

From design to output

The card, before and after

The Halloween-themed template I designed, and the final card as generated and posted. QR code blurred.

Participant posts card→GDG Ranchi & GDG India tagged→Their network sees the event→Card goes on the Networking Wall→Connections & visibility

The incentive

The Spotlight Networking Wall

Posts that used the template and tagged both pages were selected. Their cards, each with a LinkedIn QR code, went up at the venue so anyone could scan and connect. Those who couldn't attend were still there virtually.

People without a ticket still had a way to be at DevFest and get seen by speakers, attendees and sponsors, and every post grew GDG Ranchi's visibility. Skills: user-need identification, incentive design, growth loops, cross-functional leadership, idea to launch.

02  /  Work

Product thinking, shown in full.

Case studies where I start from evidence, name the real problem, and decide what to validate before building anything.

Uber pickup problem

PM case study  ·  AI Product Manager cohort  ·  Survey of 11 users

Case study
10/11Users reported difficulty meeting the driver
7/11Reported uncertainty, anxiety or worry
4/11Said the driver stopped far from the pin
4Root-cause hypotheses to validate

The problem

A confirmed ride is not yet a completed pickup

The pin and ETA are a digital reference, but the physical meetup still depends on the right entrance, a reachable stopping point and a shared landmark, especially in malls, airports and busy streets.

The framing

A coordinate can be correct and still be a poor instruction

I framed the gap between the digital point and the practical meetup point across three perspectives: the user, the driver and the platform.

Hypotheses

Location, accessibility, information, coordination

Four possible root causes to test, so the dominant failure mode is found before any solution is committed.

Decision

Measure the outcome, not one proxy

North star: successful pickup rate, with guardrails on walking distance, driver wait time and completion. Engineering investment only after a clear failure mode is validated.

The survey is directional (11 responses), and App Store reviews and Reddit threads are used only as anecdotal signals. Independent portfolio work, not affiliated with Uber.
